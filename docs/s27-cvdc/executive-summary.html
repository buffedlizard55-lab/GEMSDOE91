Thinning H19-5 onto a d=2.8 px Poisson disk (44,090 dots, GEMSDOE25/30 = 0.2600) eliminated ~63% of FP_w at near-zero TP_w loss (+0.0678 DTI). GEMSDOE31/28 (h27-4, 40,199 dots, 0.2708) pruned dots within 1.0 px of mapped faults (+0.0108 DTI). Finally, GEMSDOE32 (h33-h33-2-b2-...-zeros, 37,654 dots, 0.2778) pruned the remaining 2,545 dots within 2.0 px of existing_faults.tif—pure dead-weight false positives because organizer scoring masks visible faults and nearby pixels are already covered from >2 px. Substituting ΔFP_w = −2,545 into DTI = TP_w / (TP_w + 0.2·FP_w + 0.8·FN_w) yields TP_w ≈ 5,073 and predicts 0.2778 to within 0.0006.
