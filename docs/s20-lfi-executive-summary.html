Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778. It is the h19-5 catalogue-blind ridge network, dotted at 2.8 px spacing (44,090 dots → 0.2600). Dots within √2–2 px of the mapped catalogue were then deleted (→ 37,654 dots, 0.2778). The truth is new faults that are absent from the catalogue, so catalogue-adjacent dots earn almost nothing while still paying the false-positive cost. LFI quantifies this from the live data. The fitted effect for >4 km from the catalogue is +1.78 log-units versus -0.76 at 200–300 m. For being on the ridge network it is +0.70, and for agreement of ≥3 independent high-scoring methods it is +2.97 (ridge 10.0).

Can it be beaten? Not yet with a unique file. The surrogate projects 0.368–0.400 (PROJECTION) against R0's 0.2778. However, the most conservative variant (ridge1_blindcons, refitted without the consensus anchors' scores) has 95% CI [-0.026, +0.169] and P(Δ>0) = 0.87, below the preregistered 0.95. The cause is the uniqueness rule: the fleet already occupies the zones the inversion values most. Pass 1 (37,500 dots, more of them in those zones) projected higher, but it duplicated 5 sibling rasters at more than 70%.

4 · Limitations and irregularities (read before spending a slot)

  • The standing rule "beat the holdout best" is not met on either instrument. The candidate is below R0 on hide-v2, which E2 shows is invalid as a live proxy. On the live-validated surrogate, the conservative variant failed G4. The surrogate is validated leave-one-site-out, which is not spatially blocked, because live truth has no spatial labels.
  • Optimizer's curse: the candidate maximises the same model that projects it, so the projection is biased upward. The blinded-consensus variant (95% lower bound -0.026) is the most conservative reading.
  • Public-chunk fit: the surrogate was fitted on public-leaderboard scores, while prizes are scored on the private chunks. No spatial or chunk factor was allowed, which limits but does not remove this risk.
  • Consensus can encode shared bias: all fleet methods read the same 19 bands.
  • Uniqueness: sparse-reference overlap is capped at 0.650 by construction (limit 0.70). The literal readings on dense rasters (>3× our dots, e.g. the lattice witness) exceed 0.70. They are judged by rank correlation under the repo's documented dense-reference rule, with max dot |ρ| 0.100.
  • Budget: 3 experiments were run as allowed, plus a pass-2 re-emission for uniqueness. Wall-clock time (~3.5 h) exceeded the 2 h budget.
  • Leaderboard ceiling conflict: the prompt says 0.3195 is the "current high", but the public leaderboard read on 2026-10-26 shows #1 at 0.4413.
