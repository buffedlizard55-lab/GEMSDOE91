## 1. Verified Band Identities & Anti-Hallucination Constraints

Before formulating any hypothesis, all 19 embedded band descriptions in `data/bridge/gems-geodawn-numerical-features.tif` (`EPSG:32611`, `3730 × 3292`, 100 m pixel size) were read directly from the GeoTIFF header and cross-checked against prior byte-level repository audits (`IR-91-S12-04`, `IR-91-S13-01`, `docs/review-8e8d86f2/s23-band-semantics.json`, and DrivenData's official problem description):

1. **Band 06 (`tc`):** Embedded GeoTIFF metadata reads `'tc - Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative for edge detection'`, **however** byte-level correlation against the GeoDAWN radiometric grid (`Band 4 TC`) is $r = 0.99699$ (`IR-91-S12-04`). Band 06 is treated strictly as **airborne gamma-ray radiometric Total Count exposure**, never as a magnetic tilt angle.
2. **Band 15 (`depth_to_base_surf`):** Official DrivenData documentation defines this layer as **depth to conductive base**; it is never treated as drill-confirmed crystalline basement thickness.
3. **Band 12 (`det_elev`) & Band 19 (`det_elev_slope`):** Detrended 100 m elevation and its slope magnitude; `Band 12` is never conflated with absolute elevation or hydraulic piezometric head.
4. **NoData sentinel handling:** All non-finite values and extreme sentinels ($|v| \ge 10^{30}$, e.g. `-3.4028235e+38`) are excluded prior to every filter via masked normalized convolution (`gems91.cckm24.valid_mask`, `gems91.cckm24._masked_blur`), and valid supports are eroded beyond each operator's spatial influence radius.
5. **Target scope:** Competition labels represent expert-mapped geological faults (USGS / INGENIOUS / NBMG compilations), **not** direct observations of active geothermal vents or productive reservoirs.
