Why you saw “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” before

The competition form rejects any pixel outside [0, 1], and NaN fails that check too (NaN is not in range). Several historical GEMSDOE artifacts are -nan rasters (1.0 at dots, NaN outside the footprint) or HTML/JSON audit pages — any of these triggers the error. This repo's submission_writer has two encodings:

  • -zeros — 1.0 at dots, 0.0 everywhere else, all finite. ← this is what we submit and what the form always accepts. Validator field: form_range_check_0_1: true.
  • -nan — research-only variant matching historical experiments. Never upload this one.
Automated guarantee (no human inspection): vendor/gems57/validate.py is re-run on the exact uploaded bytes at packaging time and its full output is stored in docs/s30-vce/writer-receipt.json — including finite_everywhere, value_range_min/max, grid/transform identity, and dot placement invariants. If any check had failed, no file would have been published on this page.

What happens after submission (and what not to expect)

  • Weekly cap: submissions are limited (the exact current cap is on the submission page; the repo's standing plan assumes 2). Choose slots with the run-card evidence, not on impulse.
  • Initial Prize Round: scored against the current private labels. The incumbent family's 0.2778 is a hard target here.
  • Final Prize Round: expert-verified discoveries from submissions are added to the label set and all submissions are rescored. Unique geothermally-meaningful predictions (like this one) can gain labels; duplicates never can.
  • Check the leaderboard for the live score; report it back to the repo as an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED receipt so future sessions can use it.
Responsible-use note: this is scientific decision-support for geothermal exploration. Scores are anonymous hidden-label evaluations; they do not certify on-the-ground geothermal potential. Do not use a submission alone for lease, drilling, or investment decisions.
