**Lane (single method paragraph, parallel-run protocol §1):**

> **CBSC — Conductive-Basement Step Concordance (2026-10-26, 5A1DAF57).** On the official 100-m 19-band `gems-geodawn-numerical-features.tif` (EPSG:32611, 3730×3292, 100 m), detect coincident **conductivity and basement-depth steps** as proxies for blind faults under alluvial cover. Compute Sobel gradient magnitude of Band 17 `cond_surf` and Band 15 `depth_to_base_surf` after a 3×3 median destripe, then their **normalized product** `C = |∇cond|·|∇depth|` gated by a weak concordance requirement with Band 02 `rtp` and Band 13 `iso_grav_anom` edge strength (both gradient magnitudes). Thin via non-maximum suppression along the dominant orientation, hysteresis-threshold (high 0.85×P95, low 0.45×high), link into 8-connected components, prune < 300 m segments, and emit **32,000 binary dots** at 2.8-px minimum spacing with a strict >3-px catalogue collar. Derived catalogue-based features (distance-to-visible-faults, visible-fault density) are computed **only from visible faults** per holdout fold; all geophysical gradients use only the official bands and are label-blind. Scored with pooled DTI α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel on `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` (or `gems91-hide-recover-v1.0` if the 8 km buffer variant is unavailable). A projection is never a score.

**Why this lane, why now:** 75% of Great Basin geothermal systems are blind (Faulds & Hinz 2015, OSTI 1239344). The USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue is dominated by Quaternary scarps and compiled surface geology; faults under >100 m Quaternary fanglomerate in the Humboldt/Mud Lake playas have no scarp but offset magnetic basement and create clay-gouge conductivity contrasts (see Knowledge Base §4). S20-LFI showed `hide-v2` Spearman 0.105 vs live, so catalogue-recovery holdouts reward scarp-like signals, not blind structures — CBSC is a complementary off-catalogue-first detector inside a single assigned lane. It stays inside the lane if surface Spearman |ρ| ≤0.90 and final ≤70% dots within 3 px of any registry raster.

**Budget:** 3 experiments max (E1 CBSC-full, E2 CBSC-no-conductivity ablation, E3 CBSC-no-gravity ablation). Stop at 120 min. No weekly slot is selected here — promotion is a separate selector step.

## 1. Forensic framing: why h33-h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 and whether it can be beaten

**Evidence class:** USER-REPORTED 0.2778 vs ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED receipt — no DrivenData submission receipt binds that TIFF to 0.2778 on this branch. The GEMSDOE32 owner page itself states “NO ORGANISER SCORE EXISTS” for its artifacts (pinned commits 098f3af9, 7a97c5a8; owner text quoted in `docs/s16/sources.json` and `docs/s16/gems32-forensics.json`). The **geometry is verified** from bytes: 37,654 zeros-encoded dots, min catalogue distance 2.236 px (~224 m), median NN 2.828 px, exactly the 40,199-dot solo-d2.8 base (h27-4) minus 2,545 dots at d≤2 px. Lineage: 121,131-px h19-5 ridge network → 44,090-dot D2.8 (0.2600 CLAIM) → 40,199-dot solo-d2.8 (0.2708 CLAIM) → 37,654-dot B2 (0.2778 CLAIM). See `docs/s16/gems32-forensics.json`.

**PhD mechanism (DTI algebra, α=0.2/β=0.8, 300 m kernel):** Under organizer metric `DTI = T / [T+0.2(S-M)+0.8|G|]` where T is max-pooled kernel truth credit and S total predicted mass, sparsifying a dense 121 kpx ribbon to spaced dots removes mass that pays `0.2·FPw` without raising capped `TPw` (dense ridge → D2.8: +0.068 CLAIM). Deleting dots on mapped faults (d≤2 px) removes ~0 hidden credit because hidden truth is defined as *new* faults absent from the catalogue (forum thread 11516) and masked pixel-exactly, while still paying FPw — second gain +0.007 (→0.2778). This is **metric-geometry**, not geothermal proof; it explains why thinning and collar pruning can raise DTI without a better localizer.

**Can it be beaten — PhD judgement:** Yes, but not by re-thinning the same h19-5 surface — its implied weighted recall is only ~37% (MODEL, DENOVO §2), so rearrangements cap near +0.01–0.025. Public #1 0.4413 needs ~1.65× R0’s per-dot truth credit (≈0.62 vs 0.38 coverage if |G|≈12,500 px MODEL). That requires a **better localizer** of blind structures, not denser dots. Prior fleet shows pure ML on the 19-band stack saturates: every PU-HGB variant scored below R0 live (0.1367–0.1823). S19-SVHF de-crowded to 69% far-field >1 km and lifted SGMC >500 m +60% but holdout trailed S17-CPAD (0.1478 vs 0.1982). LFI fitted truth density to 112 live scores (LOSO Spearman 0.957) and projected ridge1 0.400 at same budget, proving live skill is recoverable but held back by uniqueness caps. A blind-under-cover signal (CBSC) is the contrarian bet that others overlook while chasing surface scarps.
