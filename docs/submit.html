What happens after you submit

  1. DrivenData scores the file against withheld truth (GEMS Prize metric DTI = T/(0.2T+0.2(S−M)+0.8|G|), triangular kernel R=3 px = 300 m at 100 m). The score appears on the leaderboard and in your submission receipt.
  2. Copy receipt_score + receipt_date into ledger/submissions.csv (same row, status=SUBMITTED) — this is the only ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED number the repo ever records.
  3. Interpret: |Δ|<0.01 = no evidence (brief §5). ≥+0.01 ⇒ that parent becomes base for next slot; ≤−0.01 ⇒ axis retired. Do not spend 3 slots on the same axis.
