Method in one paragraph

Concealed geothermal faults missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue occur as blind stepovers/relay ramps between catalogue strands and off-catalogue USGS SGMC strands, with thermal springs marking upflow. Dots are placed strictly outside the 300 m catalogue collar (catalogue faults are masked from scoring) on SGMC-strand or spring support, at ≥ 2.8 px spacing, ≤ 37,654 dots. Named non-fault mimic: unaltered lithologic contact, alluvium/playa edge, flight-line seam — mitigated by requiring the conjunction of strand proximity AND spring proximity.
