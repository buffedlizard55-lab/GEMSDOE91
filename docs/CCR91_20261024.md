## Geological method

CCR91 uses sponsor feature bands B17 `cond_surf`, B15 `depth_to_base_surf`, B13 `iso_grav_anom`, B05 `iso_grav_anom_slope`, and B19 `det_elev_slope`:

1. Estimate the broad B17-versus-B15 relation with 64 depth-quantile bins and robust conductivity medians, smoothed along depth.
2. Subtract that expectation to obtain a depth-compensated conductivity residual.
3. Derive the residual gradient and a 0.2–1.0 km difference-of-Gaussians curvature term.
4. Measure alignment between the residual gradient and the B13 gravity gradient.
5. Require B05/B13 gravity-edge corroboration and emphasize low-relief or deeper-cover terrain.
6. Add the six label-free features to the frozen S11-E2 HGB+logistic learner and unchanged two-regime submodular emitter.

The intended target is a narrow conductive or resistive discontinuity hidden within the broad basin-fill trend. The named non-fault mimic is saline groundwater or clay-rich basin fill. Residualization and gravity corroboration reduce, but do not eliminate, that ambiguity.
