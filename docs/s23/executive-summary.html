Why GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) scored highest — and how to surpass it

PhD-level DTI economics

  • Dotting is the largest reported gain (+0.0678): DTI_TP = Σ_truth max_pred k is max-pooled over 300 m; redundant pixels add FP = Σ_pred p·(1-max_truth k) linearly without TP. Spreading mass cuts FP ~64% while retaining ~95% kernel coverage.
  • Collar pruning (+0.0108+0.0070): dots 1–2 px from catalogue kernels are swallowed by masked known faults → 0.2·N denominator cost for ~0 TP.
  • Catalogue masking: known USGS/INGENIOUS faults masked; "new fault" = any pixel not in `existing_faults.tif` (DrivenData forum 11516/11536). Public leaderboard pools hidden subset.
  • Hidden depletion near catalogue: S23 re-measurement shows live truth only 45% within 1 km vs hide-and-recover 85% near. Proximity lanes scored 0.0009–0.0106 vs 0.043 random — they hurt.

How to surpass 0.2778 / 0.3195 toward 0.4413

  • R0-style spacing is saturated. Beating 0.2778 needs better localization (TP coverage 0.38 → 0.62 at same FP), not sparser dots.
  • H23-1 TILTA (tested, negative): tilt-angle edge (B06) × gravity-HG ridge (B18) aligned; targets buried magnetic-density contacts. Holdout 0.0148 shows this simple operator alone insufficient.
  • H23-2 CBPC (next, medium cost): conductivity baffle + piedmont flexure; groundwater contrasts under alluvium — strongest geothermal link.
  • High-ceiling external: USGS 3DEP 1 m LiDAR scarp U-Net (rank-5 team's disclosed pipeline) — +0.05–0.15 live expected, but requires unrestricted host for 207 GB footprint (access log).
