Dots were first spread on H19-5's 121,131-px worm network (0.1922) at 2.8 px spacing (44,090 dots, 0.2600, GEMSDOE25/30), then dots ≤1 px from catalogue removed (40,199 dots, 0.2708 — pixel-identical GEMSDOE28 H27-4 and GEMSDOE31 solo), then dots at (1,2] px removed (37,654 dots, **0.2778** GEMSDOE32). Under DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2FP + 0.8|G|) with max-per-truth-pixel credit, predictions near mapped faults are masked out of scoring and contribute almost pure 0.2·FP weight, while a second nearby prediction adds no new T credit if the kernel already covers that truth pixel. Removing redundant/collar dots thus raises DTI without changing the underlying ridge detector (closed-form predicts the next two scores within 0.001). R0's per-dot credit ≈ 0.135 at ≈38–42% weighted recall; beating ≈0.28–0.45 public #1 requires **new information**, not retuning the same ridge.
