What the session learned

Completeness weighting is the transform the *live* evidence points to (new faults are depleted near mapped faults), but a hide-and-recover holdout cannot validate it: the instrument hides whole components that sit in mapped neighbourhoods (55% of withheld truth within 1 km of visible faults), so suppressing mapped neighbourhoods suppresses the holdout's own truth. The hole-shuffled control proves the aligned field — not just any smooth reweighting — is what loses here. Two readings remain (instrument mismatch vs false mechanism) and only a live-calibrated instrument can separate them; until then the gate correctly says no. The deeper lesson stands: PU-HGB on the 19 official bands lands in the same ground every session (90.8% dot overlap with the previous lane), so the next file needs a new information source, not another transform of the same posterior.
