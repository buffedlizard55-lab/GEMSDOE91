What was tested, in one paragraph

Hypothesis (ISO91): Undiscovered faults are preferentially the short, spatially isolated tail of the fault population, so a PU model trained only on isolated catalogue components (no other component within 500 m) should recover isolated hidden faults better than the incumbent trained on all faults. Result: negative. The isolation-trained model barely moved its dots away from known faults, and with four to five times fewer positives it lost to the incumbent on both holdouts. Report-only SGMC control (more than 500 m off-catalogue): ISO91 0.071011, incumbent 0.062879, R0 0.088516. Overlap with R0's dots: 31.0% within 3 px.

Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 reached 0.2778 (USER-REPORTED): 79% of its gain over the dense ridge network came from emission geometry (2.8 px dotting) and 21% from deleting zero-credit dots within 2 px of mapped faults. Its ridges come from sub-100 m topography, and 70% of its dots lie more than 1 km from the catalogue. Two independent routes (MODEL) put the public truth set at about 12,500 px, with R0 covering about 40%. Reaching 0.4413 (PUBLIC-LEADERBOARD #1) needs about 63% more truth credit at the same number of dots. Full analysis · preregistered hypotheses · knowledge base
