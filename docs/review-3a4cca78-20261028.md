## Why the GEMSDOE32 claim may be highest; whether it can be beaten

The repository's archived lineage describes the high-scoring family as a shared ridge-support pattern with iterative dotting and pruning/thinning. Under the stated distance-weighted Tversky objective, an added prediction can contribute little additional truth credit when nearby predictions already cover the same target neighbourhood, while still increasing prediction mass and false-positive contribution. Removing redundant or near-catalogue dots can therefore improve the precision/coverage trade-off without changing the underlying ridge network. This is a plausible mechanism consistent with the archived lineage, **not proven causal attribution**: the exact score-to-file receipt, hidden labels, and organizer score decomposition are unavailable.
