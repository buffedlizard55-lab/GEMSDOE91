## 2. Detailed Physical Specification of Each Hypothesis

### `H19-1` (Rank 1): Cross-Strike Directional Semivariogram & 3-D Tensor Covariance Jump (`SVAR`)
- **Specific layers:** Fourier cardinal-notch destriped `B02` (`rtp`), `B13` (`iso_grav_anom`), `B14` (`tmi`), `B15` (`depth_to_base_surf`), and `B06` (`tc`, verified radiometric total count).
- **Physical signature / transform:** Across a buried dip-slip or transtensional fault juxtaposing dense/magnetic fractured footwall rock against porous hanging-wall basin fill, not only the mean potential field shifts — the **second-order spatial roughness (local lag-semivariogram sill $\gamma(h)$ and fractal variogram scaling slope $\frac{1}{2}\log(\gamma(600\text{ m})/\gamma(200\text{ m}))/\log 3$)** drops discontinuously from the shallow-source footwall half-plane to the upward-attenuated hanging-wall half-plane. We compute directional lag differences along $0^\circ, 45^\circ, 90^\circ, 135^\circ$ at $h \in \{200\text{ m}, 600\text{ m}\}$, extract local semivariogram anisotropy, fractal variogram exponent $H_{\text{var}}$, **cross-strike half-plane log-sill jump** $|\log(\gamma^+_{200}/\gamma^-_{200})|$ across $\pm 300\text{ m}$ normal offsets, and couple them with spectral 3-D Laplace gravity tensor tilt $\cos\theta_G$ (`z = 200 m, 600 m`) gated by Pedersen–Rasmussen quasi-2D fault linearity $Q_{2D} = 1 - |I|$.
- **Why it catches faults missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue:** Sub-alluvial horst boundaries and intra-basin step-faults buried under `100–600 m` of Quaternary fill exhibit zero surface scarp (`B12/B19` flat) and subdued first-derivative gravity amplitude where tuffs/sediments have similar bulk density, yet still produce an unmistakable sharp transition in short-wavelength magnetic/gravity spatial variance across the buried fault plane.
- **Named non-fault mimic & control:** (1) N–S / E–W airborne magnetic/radiometric flight-line leveling seams and survey block boundaries — suppressed beforehand via 2-D FFT cardinal-axis notch destriping (`IR-91-S17-LFTG-02` validated); (2) circular Tertiary plutons and caldera ring margins — suppressed by Pedersen–Rasmussen $Q_{2D} = 1 - |I|$ circular-plug rejection.
- **Difference from repo & fleet:** Prior repo lanes (`PTHA`, `S16-PGRD`, `S17-LFTG`, `S17-CPAD`) used point-wise gradients, curvature, or tensor invariants, never **opposite-half-plane directional semivariogram sill jumps or variogram fractal-exponent discontinuities** (`0` matches in `src/` and `scripts/`, `REVIEW-19912231` Priority #1 untried physical mechanism).
