## 1. The metric, verified against the organiser's own page

Official definition ([DrivenData #306 problem page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/),
fetched 2026-10-24): per truth pixel take the **max** of `p·k(d)` over
predictions within 300 m (`TPw`), false-positive mass `FPw = Σ p·(1 − max_g k)`,
`FNw = Σ (1 − max p·k)`, `DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw)`. Since
`FNw = |G| − TPw`, this is identical to `DTI = T/(0.2T + 0.2(S−M) + 0.8|G|)`
with `T = TPw` capped at 1 per truth pixel. The organiser's worked example
(`TPw 3.00, FPw 1.89, FNw 2.00 → 0.60`) reproduces exactly.

**Shared-tool bug found and fixed (IR-91-37):** `src/gems91/metric.py` summed
`p·k` instead of taking the per-truth-pixel max, so its DTI could exceed 1.
`scripts/dti.py` was already correct. The two now agree to <1e-9 on random
fields; the test that documented the symptom now asserts the official cap.

R0 ⊂ solo ⊂ d2.8 as dot sets (RECEIPT: 0 R0 dots outside solo, 0 outside
d2.8). Three effects, in order of size:

1. **Sparsification to spaced unit dots (+0.068 of the ladder).** Because
   `TPw` is capped at 1 per truth pixel, a solid 121k-pixel surface pays
   `0.2·FPw` for every pixel that is not the best cover of a truth pixel,
   while earning nothing extra. Thinning to dots ≥2.828 px apart keeps one
   kernel (R = 3 px) tiling each trace and deletes the redundant mass.
   A unit dot pays iff its marginal `TPw` gain exceeds ≈ `0.2·DTI/(1−0.2·DTI)`
   ≈ 0.059 of unmatched mass at DTI 0.2778 (MODEL, algebra above).
2. **Deleting the catalogue collar (+0.007 … +0.018).** Known-fault pixels
   are masked in scoring, so dots within ≤2 px of the catalogue mostly spend
   denominator. The two independent rungs (d2.8→R0 removes 6,436 dots,
   solo→R0 removes 2,545) imply, *if* the removed dots earned ~0 hidden
   credit, a shared hidden `TPw ≈ 5,223` and `5,470` respectively (MODEL) —
   two routes to the same number, which is why the "removed dots were pure
   cost" reading is credible.
3. **The h19-5 lineage surface itself.** With `TPw ≈ 5.3k` and `S = 37,654`,
   the implied hidden set is `|G| ≈ 13.9–14.6k` px for `M` in 3–6k (MODEL),
   i.e. R0's weighted recall is only ≈ 37%. Its ranking — multi-line
   corroborated lineaments — was simply better than every other fleet
   surface (next non-lineage fleet claims top out at 0.2376–0.2750 CLAIM).

**Can we beat 0.2778?** Marginal emission edits on the same surface are
worth at most ~+0.01–0.025 (fleet's own dose ladders; MODEL). Beating it
materially requires a better *detector*, because the public leader at
**0.4413** (LEADERBOARD-READ 2026-10-24, #1 `xiaofanhu`, 29 submissions —
[official leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/))
implies `TPw ≈ 8.5–8.9k`, i.e. ≈ 61–64% weighted recall at comparable mass
(MODEL, same `|G|` assumption) — about 1.65× R0's per-dot credit. No
rearrangement of 37,654 dots on the h19 field can manufacture that; the
leader's surface knows about faults ours does not. The one sponsor-provided
data source this fleet has not used at full fidelity is the **1 m DEM**
(experts map scarps from topography; the competition supplies the download
links) — see hypothesis H4 and IR-91-40.

## 4. Holdout (spatially blocked, whole-segment, pooled official DTI)

Protocol: catalogue connected components assigned whole to 4 checkerboard
block folds (400 px blocks); per fold the >3 px exclusion is derived from
the *visible* components only; supervised models train only outside the fold
region; visible catalogue is never in the truth; DTI pooled over folds
(`scripts/dti.py`, α 0.2 / β 0.8 / R 3 px). Withheld positives: 60,988 px
(15,105 / 15,978 / 12,874 / 17,031 per fold). Emission identical for every
de novo method: greedy surface order, 2.8 px min spacing, 37,654-dot budget.

Caveat that changes the reading: d2.8-as-is is **leakage-flattered** — its
construction used the full catalogue (8,607 of its dots sit ≤3 px from it,
earning withheld credit no honest fold method may use) and it spends 44,090
dots against our 37,654. On the like-for-like per-dot credit, HGB-CTX is
ahead (0.2168 vs 0.2077). HGB-CTX beat the previous honest holdout best
(SUP) by +0.0457 pooled with essentially non-overlapping fold CIs, so it —
and only it — was built as a submission.

**Leakage canary:** every single sponsor band alone has AUC ≤ 0.5779
(best `geod_2ndinv` 0.5777, `ieq_n100a15` 0.5764; 60,988 catalogue px vs
150,000 negatives >5 px off-catalogue) — all far below the 0.90 leakage
bar. The lidar diagnostic bands measure 0.52–0.55 pixelwise here
(`valid` coverage only 75% of footprint); radiometrics 0.43–0.47 raw.
Pixelwise AUC understates line-like detectors, which is why the DTI
holdout, not AUC, is the promotion instrument.

**The disagreement we will not hide:** on the independent USGS SGMC
off-catalogue control (62,122 px, report-only PROXY), our file scores
0.068703 vs R0's 0.095386. Converting each instrument's per-dot credit

beating R0's 0.2778 CLAIM is possible at the top of the range and *not*
