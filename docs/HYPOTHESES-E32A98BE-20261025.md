## Scientific limits and sources

- **Topographic roughness is not a direct fault detector.** Candela et al. (2012) measured scale-dependent, slightly anisotropic self-affine roughness on exhumed fault surfaces and earthquake ruptures. Their result motivates a test but does not validate a 100 m detrended-DEM texture statistic; the proposed raster signature is a separate, uncertain geomorphic proxy. [Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth, DOI 10.1029/2011JB009041](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2011JB009041).
- **Semivariogram support is analogical, not transfer evidence.** Nakaya et al. (2002) estimate directional semivariograms of a 3-D rock-permeability field and report greater correlation parallel to fault planes in a dam-site case study. Airborne potential fields are different observables and require an independent holdout test. [Water Resources Research, DOI 10.1029/2001WR000672](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2001WR000672).
- A USGS Nevada Landsat study describes major image lineaments as morphological/tonal expressions of broad structural zones; it does **not** validate Landsat thermal anomalies as fault truth. [USGS OFR 79-544](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/geologic-evaluation-major-landsat-lineaments-nevada-and-their-relationship-ore).
- The USGS SGMC page describes a generalized compilation (48 state maps, with unreconciled boundaries and bedrock-only representation in states with both bedrock and surficial maps). It is a conditioning/control layer, not ground truth for hidden faults. [USGS 2026 GeMS-SGMC metadata](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geologic-map-schema-gems-version-state-geologic-map-compilation-sgmc-geodatabase-conterminous) · [NGMDB GIS download record](https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Prodesc/proddesc_119417.htm).
- USGS product-level pages: [Landsat Collection 2 Surface Temperature](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-temperature), [Landsat Level-2 products](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-level-2-science-products), and [3DEP products/services](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services). A public product page is not proof that exact scenes or tiles are available over this project at useful quality.
