## 1. Why 0.2778 won the fleet, and what beating it requires

`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` (0.2778 **CLAIM** — no
organiser receipt in this repo; the GEMSDOE32 page itself records no score
receipt, see `docs/DENOVO_20261024.md` §2) is a 37,654-dot strict subset of
solo-d2.8 (40,199) and dotted-h19-5 (44,090), verified from the pinned
rasters. Under the official DTI (α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel,
per-truth-pixel **max** credit; DrivenData #306 problem page
<https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/>):

1. **Sparsification** to ≥ 2.828 px spaced unit dots removed solid-surface
   mass that paid `0.2·FPw` while adding nothing to the capped `TPw`
   (largest rung of the CLAIM ladder, 0.1922 → 0.2600).
2. **Catalogue-collar deletion** removed dots that cannot earn hidden
   credit (mapped pixels are masked in scoring) (+0.007 rung).
3. The **h19-5 detector surface** underneath was the fleet's best ranking,
   yet its implied weighted recall is only ≈ 37 % (MODEL). Re-emitting the
   same field is therefore capped at roughly +0.01–0.025 (MODEL); the
   leaderboard head (0.4413, leaderboard read recorded in DENOVO §2 on
   2026-10-24) implies ≈ 1.65× R0's per-dot credit. **Beating 0.2778
   materially requires a detector that sees faults the fleet's surfaces
   miss — not another rearrangement of R0 dots.**

The fleet's detectors are overwhelmingly symmetric: ridge/crest filters,
gradient magnitudes, tilt derivatives, and (HGB-CTX) raw pixel values plus
symmetric 11×11 means. A fault's most basic signature is none of those — it
is a **level step across the trace** (footwall up / hanging-wall down in
elevation, basement depth, density, magnetisation). A symmetric mean centred
on a step returns the average of the two sides; the step itself is cancelled.
ASC91 tests exactly that blind spot.

## 2. What was built (single lane, 3 frozen experiments)

Odd-symmetric step response per field (bands 12 `det_elev`, 13
`iso_grav_anom`, 2 `rtp`, 15 `depth_to_base_surf`, 17 `cond_surf`; names
verified from the stack's own tags) at 8 strike azimuths:
`[(F(+2n)+F(+4n)) − (F(−2n)+F(−4n))]/2`, smoothed along strike
(`src/gems91/asc.py`, unit-tested on synthetic steps/ridges in
`tests/test_asc.py`). Features per pixel: step-magnitude and directional
anisotropy percentiles (×5), signed steps at the cross-field modal azimuth
oriented footwall-positive by elevation (×5), modal-azimuth agreement,
axial azimuth (sin 2θ, cos 2θ) — 18 total, no distance-to-catalogue feature.
Emission identical for every arm: greedy spaced dots, 2.8 px, 37,654 budget,
> 3 px from the visible catalogue (`gems91.denovo.greedy_spaced` reused).

- **E1 ASC-U:** unsupervised geometric-mean concordance of the 5 step
  magnitudes × azimuth-agreement gate.
- **E2 ASC-S:** HistGradientBoosting (repo `_train_hgb` hyper-parameters
  reused verbatim) on the 18 step features only.
- **E3 (frozen branch, E2 > E1):** E2 + TRI3 surface percentile (TRI3
  rebuilt by the reused `build_denovo.load_surface`).

Non-fault mimic (named): lithologic/intrusive contacts and Lake Lahontan
shoreline terraces are also cross-line steps; a buried volcanic margin can
step elevation, gravity, magnetics and basement depth together.

## 3. Results — identical-instrument holdout (all HOLDOUT-DTI)

Instrument: `build_denovo.fold_setup` (whole 8-connected catalogue
components → 4 checkerboard block folds; per-fold exclusion from visible
components only; supervised training outside the fold region) scored pooled
by `scripts/dti.py` (official DTI). Withheld positives reproduced exactly:
15,105 / 15,978 / 12,874 / 17,031 = **60,988**. The random control

### Reading (judgement, labelled MODEL where inferential)

- **Step information is real.** E2 (18 step features from 5 level fields)
  beats the 20-feature raw-percentile logistic model (0.1155 vs 0.0971) and
  reaches 81 % of HGB-CTX's per-dot credit while using none of the
  strain/seismicity/terrain-correction bands that HGB-CTX exploits. E1 ≈ TRI3
  unsupervised (0.0901 vs 0.0912).
- **It is not sufficient alone.** E3 adds only +0.0013 over E2 (fold CIs
  overlap almost completely), and E3 remains 0.026 below HGB-CTX pooled.
  The step view and the raw/context view overlap in what they recover on
  *catalogue-like* (mostly exposed) withheld faults; the holdout cannot
  measure the buried-fault regime where the step mechanism should help
  most (withheld segments are drawn from the same exposure-biased
  catalogue — a known instrument limitation, cf. SLOT_PLAN IR-91-S10-04).
- **The obvious fusion (ASC features + HGB-CTX features) was deliberately
  not run:** it is a 4th experiment (budget = 3), a different lane, and its
  surface would need a fresh novelty check against the DENOVO raster.
  It is the top recommendation for the next session (§6), with the
  preregistered expectation that near-orthogonal step features
  (surface |ρ| ≤ 0.1374 vs every checked registry raster here) are the
  cheapest plausible route past 0.142885 HOLDOUT-DTI — a MODEL expectation,
  not a score.

## 5. Irregularities and limitations flagged (no silent fixes)

1. **IR-91-ASC-01 (instrument):** `fold_setup` assigns components by
   centroid; a withheld component may extend outside its fold region and
   still counts in pooled G, and supervised negatives are drawn > 5 px from
   the *visible* catalogue outside the region. Kept byte-identical to the
   DENOVO instrument for comparability; affects all arms on it equally.
2. **IR-91-ASC-02 (transform):** percentile/robust-z normalisations are fit
   on the full footprint (unsupervised, label-free; same pattern as DENOVO's
   global reference sample). Canary AUCs ≤ 0.6028 show no label leakage.
3. **IR-91-ASC-03 (registry scope):** the near-universal lattice raster
   that blocked CED (`calib_r13lattice_00904.tif`, 99.87 % footprint
   coverage within 3 px) is absent from this restored registry; under the
   literal directed rule it would reject any candidate. No exemption was
   applied — the 11 checked files are enumerated with hashes in the build
   evidence. A fleet-universal registry still does not exist in this repo.
4. **IR-91-ASC-04 (boundary):** sponsor bands are sentinel-filled by
   nearest-valid EDT, so steps within ~4 px of the footprint edge use
   extrapolated values (shared with the DD-VTC lane's fill).
5. **Scores in the brief** (0.2778, 0.3195, 0.4413, fleet table) remain
   CLAIM / leaderboard-read values with no receipts in this repo; none was
   used as a gate. The only gates were same-instrument HOLDOUT-DTI,
   canary, novelty and format.
6. **No new external data was used.** The highest-ceiling untried source
   (USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM via the competition's DEM links / The National Map —
   free and official) is not reachable from this sandbox (egress limited to
   github/pypi/npm) and the pinned derived lidar copy's provenance is
   unverified (DENOVO IR-91-40), so it is documented as blocked-here, not
   proposed as viable in-session.
