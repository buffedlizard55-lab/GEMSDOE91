### Why the reported GEMSDOE32 0.2778 may be high — and what is not established

The mechanism is plausible, not proven: with β greater than α, missed truth carries greater nominal penalty than false-positive mass. A spatial metric with saturating nearest-prediction credit can also make redundant adjacent pixels inefficient. Removing weak or redundant predicted mass may improve precision while retaining coverage. The official formula alone cannot tell us that a particular GEMSDOE32 TIFF scored 0.2778, why it scored that value, or whether the same rule transfers to unseen faults. The public/private target is new, expert-identified faults absent from the mapped USGS catalogue, whereas the repository’s hide-and-recover exercise withholds catalogue components. That holdout is useful for controlled model comparisons but is not a direct simulation of private unseen-fault performance.

**Answer:** exceeding a USER-REPORTED 0.2778 is possible in principle; this review supplies no evidence that this checkout can do so. The current public global top is a different PUBLIC-LEADERBOARD observation. A holdout projection must never be called an organizer score.
