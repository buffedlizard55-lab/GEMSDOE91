### 0.2 Exact Byte-Verified Mechanism of `0.2778` (`GEMSDOE32` `h33-h33-2-b2-zeros.tif`)
Byte-exact forensics on the SHA-256-pinned lineage (`registry/data_manifest.json` & `evidence/s14_winner_forensics.json`) establish that `h33-h33-2-b2` (`37,654` binary `{0, 1}` dots, `sha256: c55bafc470054e82…`) achieved `0.2778` (`CLAIM`) via **three coupled mechanisms**:

1. **Dinkelbach Binary-Vertex & Max-Kernel Submodularity (`+0.0678` from `0.1922 -> 0.2600`):**
   Official Distance-Tolerant Intersection (`scripts/dti.py`, matching `drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution` commit `aebe92f7c8`) evaluates
   $$\mathrm{DTI}(p, G) = \frac{T(p, G)}{\alpha\,T(p, G) + \alpha\,(S - M) + \beta\,|G|}, \quad \alpha = 0.2,\;\beta = 0.8,\;K(r) = \max\!\left(0,\,1 - \frac{r}{300\text{ m}}\right)$$
   where truth credit $T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_j p_j K(\|g - j\|)$ uses a **pointwise `max` kernel**, whereas false-positive penalty $\alpha(S - M)$ sums total prediction mass $S = \sum_j p_j$ linearly.
   - *Binary optimality:* Scaling $p \mapsto c\,p$ for $0 < c < 1$ scales $T$ and $S - M$ identically by $c$ while $\beta |G|$ stays fixed, strictly decreasing $\mathrm{DTI}$. Optimal predictions lie at extreme vertices $p_j \in \{0, 1\}$.
   - *Submodular saturation:* Along a 1-D fault trace, placing contiguous unit pixels (`121,131` px in `19GEMSDOE` `h19-5`, `0.1922 CLAIM`) saturates $T$ after every third pixel while tripling $\alpha S$. Thinning the **identical** `h19-5` ridge field to `d = 1.5 px` (`60,069` dots, `0.2477 CLAIM`) and `d = 2.8 px` (`44,090` dots, `0.2600 CLAIM`) lifted the score by `+0.0678` with zero change to the underlying geological detector.

2. **Catalogue-Collar Tax Elimination (`+0.0178` from `0.2600 -> 0.2708 -> 0.2778`):**
   Because mapped faults in `existing_faults.tif` (`60,988` pixels) are masked out of the hidden test truth $G_{\text{hidden}}$, dots placed on or immediately beside mapped traces lie in already-mapped corridors where unmapped parallel strands are rare, yet each dot still pays $+\alpha = 0.2$ to the denominator. Pruning `dotted-d2.8` at $B = 1\text{ px}$ (`100 m`, `40,199` dots -> `0.2708 CLAIM` in `GEMSDOE28`) and $B = 2\text{ px}$ (`200 m`, `37,654` dots -> `0.2778 CLAIM` in `GEMSDOE32`) removed `6,436` near-catalogue dots (`69.8%` of remaining dots lie `> 1 km` from mapped faults).
