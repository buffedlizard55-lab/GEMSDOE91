## Why might GEMSDOE32's reported 0.2778 be highest here?

Byte-level support comparison establishes the R0 family relationship, not score causality: a 44,090-dot D2.8 reference contains a 40,199-dot subset after **3,891** deletions within one pixel of the catalogue; that subset contains the 37,654-dot R0 after a further **2,545** deletions between one and two pixels. No R0 dots were added. Given the [official distance-weighted Tversky metric](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric), redundant or low-credit predictions can increase weighted false-positive mass without proportionate truth-side coverage. Spacing and pruning are therefore plausible explanations for a reported improvement. They do **not** establish the hidden labels, prove why the reported score changed, authenticate 0.2778, or justify a broad collar.

[DrivenData staff](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4) says known faults are masked **pixel-exactly**, new truth may lie within 300 m, and nearby unsupported predictions can be penalized. Thus pruning adjacent predictions can also remove genuine splays or mapping corrections. Per [staff's aggregation clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/leaderboard-aggregation-pooled-over-public-test-pixels-or-mean-of-per-chunk-scores/11550/2), public and private results are pooled over their respective subsets, and final reevaluation is over the full region; hidden subset geometry is undisclosed. Historic inverse-score estimates of hidden truth are not validated by a pair of reported scores.

The official [public leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/) showed rank 1 **0.4544** on 27 October 2026, above the user's earlier 0.4413 snapshot and the user's claimed 0.2778. It demonstrates that a higher public result exists, not that any project candidate can reproduce it or that a specific predicted vent exists. The cause of the best result and feasibility of crossing 0.2778 for our own next file remain unproven.
