Why the champion scores highest of the family

Under DTI (α=0.2 FP penalty, β=0.8 FN penalty, 300 m kernel) the B=2 shell removal deletes mostly-unmatched clutter (cutting FP cost) while the retained dots still reach hidden faults near catalogue strands (keeping TP credit) — GEMSDOE91's R0 byte-lineage analysis measured the 0.2600→0.2778 gain as pure pruning of near-catalogue dots. The margin over the runner-up family variants is exactly this. Critical implication: the pruning lever is now exhausted — more shell removal loses coverage faster than it saves FP. The only remaining route to a higher score is new hidden-fault coverage: dots on real but uncatalogued structure, placed by an independent signal, each dot earning more than the α·DTI≈0.0556 break-even it costs.
