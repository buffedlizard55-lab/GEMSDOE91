## Why the reported GEMSDOE32 `0.2778` might be high—and what cannot be concluded

A plausible mechanism is **emission efficiency under a spatial, saturating DTI objective**: deleting predictions with little marginal truth credit can lower false-positive mass while preserving much of the kernel-weighted coverage. A 300 m triangular kernel may make nearby redundant predictions contribute diminishing incremental credit. A catalogue-distance filter could also change the tradeoff on a hide-and-recover benchmark.

That is a hypothesis about score mechanics, not a demonstrated cause of the reported `0.2778`. A single reported value cannot isolate the effect of dot count, spacing, catalogue pruning, the scoring split, or artifact identity. It also cannot establish blind-fault or geothermal-vent discovery. A matched ablation needs the same locked surface, split, holdout evaluator, emission budget and confidence procedure; only an organizer receipt authenticates the competition result. The repository's forensic notes report that the named R0 dot set is a pruned form of a base set, but this review did not freshly rebuild that analysis because its input rasters are absent.
