## Interpretation of the reported GEMSDOE32 0.2778

The supplied 0.2778 is not evidence, by itself, for a fault-detection model that beats another model. The repo's forensic notes attribute the gain to a sparse “dotted” emission derived from a ridge/lineament surface and successive deletion of dots near known traces. Under a mass-sensitive spatial DTI, a prediction can add false-positive mass while bringing little new truth coverage once nearby truth is already served. Removing redundant or low-marginal-credit dots can thus improve the metric without improving the underlying ranking surface. A catalogue collar can also trade away little marginal coverage for less penalized mass.

That is a plausible metric mechanism, **not a verified causal decomposition**: the cited score is not accompanied here by an organizer receipt, and independent runs/splits must not be compared as if they were matched. Establish the effect with a same-surface, same-fold, same-budget ablation (dense surface → dots → each collar increment) on the shared evaluator. A hidden-catalogue holdout measures recovery of withheld catalogue traces, not independently confirmed unmapped faults or geothermal vents. No holdout projection is a leaderboard score.
