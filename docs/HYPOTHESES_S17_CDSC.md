### 0.2 Byte-Verified Mathematical Mechanism of `0.2778` (`GEMSDOE32` `R0`)
Direct byte-level forensics on the SHA-256-pinned lineage (`registry/data_manifest.json` & `docs/s16/gems32-forensics.json`) and the official Distance-Tolerant Intersection (`DTI`) metric (`drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution` commit `aebe92f7c8a990f0e3443451b7a825d9afd6336b`, `scripts/dti.py`) establish that `DTI` is governed by:
$$\mathrm{DTI}(p, G) = \frac{T(p, G)}{\alpha\,T(p, G) + \alpha\,\big(S(p) - M(p, G)\big) + \beta\,|G|}, \quad \alpha = 0.2,\;\beta = 0.8,\;K(r) = \max\!\left(0,\,1 - \frac{r}{300\text{ m}}\right)$$
where truth coverage $T(p, G) = \sum_{g \in G} \max_j p_j K(\|g - j\|_2)$ uses a **pointwise `max` kernel**, whereas false-positive penalty $\alpha(S(p) - M(p, G))$ sums total emitted mass $S(p) = \sum_j p_j$ linearly. `h33-h33-2-b2` (`37,654` binary `{0, 1}` dots) rose from `0.1922` (`19GEMSDOE` `h19-5`, `121,131` px) through `0.2600` (`GEMSDOE25` `d2.8`, `44,090` dots) and `0.2708` (`GEMSDOE28` `solo-d2.8`, `40,199` dots) to `0.2778` (`CLAIM`) via three exact mechanisms:
1. **Dinkelbach Binary-Vertex Optimality (`p_j in {0, 1}`):** Scaling any fractional surface $p \mapsto c\,p$ ($0 < c < 1$) scales $T$ and $S - M$ by $c$ while $\beta|G| > 0$ remains constant, strictly lowering $\mathrm{DTI}$. Optimal predictions lie at extreme binary vertices $\{0, 1\}^N$, encoded in all-finite `-zeros.tif` (`nodata=None`) to prevent DrivenData's *"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"* portal error.
2. **Submodular 1-D Kernel Decimation (`~2.5–2.8 px` spacing):** Because $T$ takes a pointwise `max` over a $300\text{ m}$ ($3\text{ px}$) cone, contiguous 1-D ridge pixels saturate $T$ while paying $3\times$ the $\alpha = 0.2$ mass tax.
