1 · Why h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 (measured, this repo)

Re-scored with the shared evaluator on sha256-pinned rasters (full table): across 7 fleet ancestors, organizer CLAIM scores correlate with dot count ρ = −1.000, spacing ρ = +0.898 and far-field share (>1 km from catalogue) ρ = +0.857, while local holdout and SGMC proxies anti-correlate (ρ = −0.75 / −0.82). Mechanism: under DTI's max-kernel, contiguous lines waste mass (T saturates, S is taxed at α = 0.2); thinning one surface 121k → 37,654 dots at ≈3 px spacing climbed 0.1922 → 0.2778. Hidden faults also cannot sit in the catalogue's mapped halo, so winners spend 59–70% of dots >1 km away. Beating 0.2778 therefore needs a better far-field surface at the same emission economics — exactly what S14 adds: two sensor families no prior lane used.
