Method in one paragraph

DEF4A160-heuristic uses only the SHA-pinned 19-band GeoDAWN stack (no external raster). Four rank features feed one geometric-mean surface (no ML in this lite variant): (1) gradient magnitude of detrended elevation B12, (2) gradient magnitude of basement depth B15, (3) scaling-break ratio var(15)/var(7) of B12 (masked local variance) as log1p rank, and (4) basement-step / strain coincidence sqrt(mag15·mag04)·azimuth-agreement rank where B15's depth gradient aligns within 22.5° of B04's geodetic second-invariant gradient. The fusion targets faults missing from the catalogue because they are buried under bajada/Lahontan fill: the hanging-wall/footwall texture differs at 50–300 m scale even without a sharp scarp, and the basement step still coincides with a strain concentration. Cross-field azimuth agreement and the pairwise scale-contrast (sampled ±7 px across the local gradient normal) suppress unfaulted contacts. Training labels (catalogue) enter only as holdout truth and as the emission collar; no catalogue-distance feature is used. Named mimics: lithologic contacts, fan-lobe margins, Lake Lahontan shorelines, roads/vegetation steps (scale break) and volcanic/compaction steps (basement). 20,000 dots are emitted by shared NMS at 3.5 px spacing / 5.0 px collar. A fault map is not evidence of a vent.
