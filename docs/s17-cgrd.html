IR-91-S15-06 & IR-91-S15-07 Remediation Proof

In S17-CGRD, every pairwise 2-quadrant and 3-quadrant OOF Stage-1 surface is calibrated via gems91.ptha.prior_shift_calibrate_surface using its exact training-domain base rate (pi_true ≈ 0.0118, pi_sample = 0.1667) before stitching, and ≥ 3.2 km regional bowls are subtracted. As a result, E2 improves strictly over E1 (+0.008144 HOLDOUT-DTI) while remaining 100% invariant to topographic bands B12 and B19.
