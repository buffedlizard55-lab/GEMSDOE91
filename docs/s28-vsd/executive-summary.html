8) Ranked hypotheses tested (from the frozen preregistration)

  1. CDEC — cross-domain (gravity×magnetics) multi-scale edge concordance. Layers B02/B13, scales 300/600/1200 m. Catches faults the map misses because both physical contrasts persist even where mapping is silent. New: no prior repo module required two-field azimuth agreement.
  2. TSR — relay/stepover gap morphology (binary closing of the concordant-edge support at r=3/6/10 px). Targets the bridges where Coulomb stress transfer says hidden segments live. New: no prior module scored inter-lineament gaps.
  3. STA — seismo-tectonic alignment (B16 density × B10 proximity, coupled to edge azimuth). New as an azimuth-coupling feature.
  4. Stage-2 nested auto-context on the widened 53-feature stack (template machinery).
  5. Emission policy — submodular 300 m-kernel CELF vs greedy NMS at fixed 37,654-dot budget (preregistered paired arm).
