**Metric mechanics (verified from [problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and [NLR rules](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf)):** DTI = TPw/(TPw+0.2 FPw+0.8 FNw) with R=300 m triangular kernel k(d)=max(0,1−d/3). Because TPw is a *max* over predictions within 300 m of each truth pixel, narrow 1-px dot traces achieve near-identical TPw to 3-px ribbons while paying 1/3 the FPw mass (0.2 weight). GEMSDOE32's sparse, well-separated dots maximize credit per dot.
