## 2. Why R0 (`h33-h33-2-b2…-zeros`, 0.2778 USER-REPORTED CLAIM) led the fleet

Measured (pinned sha `c55bafc4…`): 37,654 unit dots, NN spacing min/med
2.828/3.0 px, catalogue distance p0/p50/p95 = 2.236/19.6/81.9 px, ≤3 px
coverage 15.09% of footprint. Mechanism (MODEL on the verified metric):
(1) per-truth-pixel **max** credit saturates, so thinning a ~121k-px surface
to ≥2.828 px-spaced dots deletes denominator mass (0.2·FPw) at ~zero credit
cost — fleet ladder 0.1922 → 0.2477 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 tracks exactly
this sparsification; (2) catalogue-collar deletion removes dots that cannot
earn hidden-truth credit; (3) the h19-5 ranking surface underneath was the
fleet's best detector — every top fleet file is a rearrangement of it, and
rearrangements span only ~0.033. Beating 0.2778 materially (leader 0.4413 ⇒
~1.65× R0 weighted recall at similar mass) requires a better *detector*, not
better emission. No organizer receipt for 0.2778 exists in this repo
(IR-91-S10-05), so it stays a CLAIM. **Can we beat it? Unknown without a
receipt; this session's candidate does not beat the local holdout bar and is
therefore not promoted (below).**
