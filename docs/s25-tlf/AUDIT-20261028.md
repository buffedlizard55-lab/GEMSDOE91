## Historical champion and proposed hypotheses

Among the nine user-provided historical claims the top is GEMSDOE32 `h33-h33-2-b2` (0.2778, **user-reported; no receipt**). Its [source review](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32) describes a related sequence of spatial thinning and near-catalogue pruning. Under the distance-weighted Tversky metric, redundant predictions can add false-positive mass without adding much coverage. That is a plausible mechanism, **not demonstrated causation**: hidden labels and score decomposition are unavailable, and the edits are correlated. A higher score is possible in principle, but no honest estimate or evidence of improvement is available here.
