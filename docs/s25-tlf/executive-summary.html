Why the nine spread from 0.2449 to 0.2778 (and what that means)

  • They are one raster, edited. Every leader has ≥97.6% of its dots within 3 px of another leader. Nine submissions, one hypothesis: the “dotted ridge” family.
  • The metric explains the ordering. DTI = T / (0.2·(T+FP) + 0.8·|G|). Stacked dots do not add truth credit — the numerator takes the maximum kernel weight per truth pixel — but each wasted dot adds full false-positive mass. So once a ribbon is covered, extra dots can only hurt. That is exactly the observed pattern exactly: the four smallest rasters (37,654; 37,660; 38,854; 40,199 dots) claim the four highest scores, and the two largest (60,069; 61,328) claim the two lowest. Spearman(claimed score, dot count) over the nine = -0.967 (computed in nine-site-table.json; n=9, unverified claims — a consistent sign, not proof of causality).
  • What it does not tell us. No receipt decomposes T, FP and |G|, so the cause of the 0.03 spread cannot be isolated from here, and three of the nine share identical support, so effective n≈3, not 9.

Method in one paragraph

Hypothesis: catalogued-absent faults in the Great Basin retain a topographic expression (scarps, composite ridges, faceted spurs) after their potential-field signature has healed. Layers used: band 19 (det_elev_slope), band 6 (tc, tilt/total-curvature), band 2 (rtp, reduced-to-pole magnetic), and band 8 of the independent USGS/GeoDAWN lidar scarp features stack (cross_max). Each layer is converted to a structure-tensor lineament response (gradient magnitude weighted by coherence, so isotropic relief is suppressed), rank-normalised inside the scored footprint, and fused (magnetic/tilt/slope weighted by their measured excess AUC, 50/50 with the lidar layer). Dots are placed greedily best-first with a 2.8-px Poisson-disc separation — because the metric takes the maximum kernel weight per truth pixel, so stacked mass buys nothing and costs false-positive weight — and no dot is placed within 3.05 px of a mapped trace. Mimic process guarded against: playa margins, terrace risers and shorelines; the leakage canary is in the run card (max single-feature AUC 0.689, floor from a seeded random field 0.500).
