Why GEMSDOE32 (0.2778) scored highest — and how to surpass it

Why it scored highest (DTI economics)

  • Dotting is the largest gain (+0.0678): TP = Σ_truth max_y p(y)·k(d) is max-pooled over a 3 px disk, so redundant pixels add no TP but FP = Σ_y p(y)(1 − max_g k) sums linearly. Spreading mass cuts FP ~64% while keeping ~95% of kernel tube coverage.
  • Collar pruning (+0.0108 +0.0070): dots 1–2 px from the catalogue have kernels mostly swallowed by masked known-fault pixels, paying 0.2·S denominator for ~0 TP. Removing them raises DTI.
  • Catalogue masking: known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked from scoring (DrivenData forum 11516/11536). A "new fault" is any pixel not in the catalogue, so dots on known faults earn zero credit.
  • Hidden faults depleted near mapped faults: live truth is 55% within 1 km of visible faults vs holdout; pure proximity lanes scored 0.0009–0.0106 vs 0.052 random.

How to surpass 0.2778 / 0.3195 toward 0.4413

  • R0-style dotting economics are saturated. Beating 0.2778 needs better localisation (coverage 0.38 → 0.62 at same FP), not just sparser emitters.
  • H-S18-01 VAD (top candidate): concealed displacement offsets basement lithology, changing spatial covariance (magnetic/gravity texture) under 0.3–2.5 km Quaternary fill where absolute field edge is weak. Basin-stripped Bouguer curvature isolates 2-D horst steps; RTP gradient isolates magnetite-destruction lows. Their product separates fault steps from equidimensional volcanic plugs and regional lithologic gradients.
  • H-S18-02 GCD: SGMC-conditioned gravity dipole catches faults internal to a map unit (no lithologic colour contrast → missed by geologic mapping).
  • H-S18-05 LST: multi-season Landsat thermal residual (direct heat, blind upflow) — highest ceiling but blocked in this sandbox (needs USGS EarthExplorer / AWS landsat-pds download).

Honest caveat: 0.2778 is USER-REPORTED, UNVERIFIED. No organizer receipt exists. Holdout-DTI cannot be directly compared to live leaderboard score (instrument mismatch: holdout truth is near mapped faults; live truth is depleted near them). A projection is never a score.
