4 · What BSD91 is (new, not copied from any GEMSDOE site)

Hypothesis: concealed geothermal faults missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue occur as blind stepovers/relay ramps between catalogue strands and off-catalogue USGS SGMC strands, with thermal springs marking upflow; permeability is hosted in the 300 m–1 km damage zone.

Method: dots placed strictly outside the 300 m catalogue collar (3 < d_catalogue <= 12 px — catalogue faults are masked from scoring, so dots on mapped traces waste false-positive mass) AND within 500 m of an off-catalogue SGMC strand or 600 m of a Hot/Warm GDR spring; 4,021 binary dots on an 800 m deterministic lattice, ≥ 2.8 px spacing (beyond the 300 m kernel so dots do not self-cancel), capped at 37,654 dots.

Named non-fault mimic: unaltered lithologic contact, alluvium/playa edge, flight-line seam — mitigated by requiring the conjunction of strand proximity AND spring proximity.

Why new: no training_features.tif bands (unlike PGRD/CWPC/CPAD/CDSC/EULR/DRN91/SNAP/RELIEF); no parent-submission derivation (unlike PRUNE/CONCENTRATION); annulus-plus-fault-support (unlike DZEDGE annulus-only, measured 0.0 deliberate negative). All inputs are pinned and locally verified.

6 · Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778 (user-reported, unverified)

Byte-exact lineage from pinned ancestors (h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e): h19-5 ridge network (0.1922) → Poisson-disk dotting at kernel spacing (0.2600: same 300 m kernel coverage at ~36% of the mass, since T takes a pointwise max over the 300 m disk while false-positive mass is linear) → speckle pruning (0.2708) → deletion of 2,545 dots at √2–2.0 px from the catalogue, leaving 37,654 dots (0.2778). Beating it requires a better localiser of catalogue-missing faults, not more pruning of the same dots. Full analysis: docs/HYPOTHESES_S17_CWPC.md §2.
