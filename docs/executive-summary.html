GEMSDOE32: what the archived evidence does and does not prove

The historical byte audit found R0 = its 40,199-dot parent minus 2,545 dots within 200 m of mapped faults, with no additions. This verifies a geometric operation, not its hidden-score effect. Under the requested DTI formula, T/(0.2T+0.2(S−M)+0.8|G|), deleting a dot changes both truth credit T and predicted mass S−M. Even when hidden truth excludes mapped fault pixels, it can lie within the 300 m kernel of a near-catalogue dot. Such a dot is NOT necessarily pure penalty. Pruning may help or hurt; matched ablations and organizer receipts are required to attribute a gain.
