Why the GEMSDOE32 claim (0.2778) is the family’s highest — and its limits

Across the nine byte-identical-audited leaders, claimed values rise as positive-pixel count falls from ≈60k toward ≈37.6k (Spearman ρ(dots, claim) = -0.983 over these nine non-independent files). That is consistent with the official metric (problem description: α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel): within one shared support, pruning/redundantly spaced dots removes false-positive weight while neighbours within 300 m keep covering the same truth pixels. Causality is not proven — the claims have no receipts, the files overlap by ≥93–100%, and one score cannot separate TP/FP/FN. The same pruning effect appears in our paired data in reverse: raising the CCKM budget from 20,000 to 37,654 gained +0.029944 HOLDOUT-DTI (95% CI [0.026266, 0.033683]) because coverage, not novelty, was the binding term at 20k.

Can this lane beat 0.2778? On current evidence, no: at the identical 37,654-dot budget the CCKM surface reaches 57.1% of the GEMSDOE32 raster on the SGMC off-catalogue diagnostic and adds nothing significant over a plain gravity-gradient ranking in paired holdout. Beating 0.2778 likely requires a materially better surface (supervised multi-band mapping, the official 1 m DEM links, diverse ensembling) validated on a live-analogue instrument — not another emission edit of this surface. That is an expert judgement from the two labelled diagnostics, not an estimated score.

How a future, selector-approved submission would be made

  1. Pass every gate above, then obtain a separate selector decision (this page cannot grant one) and check the remaining weekly quota on the authenticated submission page (the website limit prevails over the rules PDF).
  2. Download the approved -zeros.tif (or its one-TIFF ZIP) and verify its SHA-256 locally (sha256sum file.tif on Linux, Get-FileHash file.tif in PowerShell) against the run card before uploading. All-finite values in [0,1] are what prevent the portal error “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”: the writer rejects sentinels such as -3.4028235e+38 instead of copying them.
  3. Upload the single-band GeoTIFF (EPSG:32611, float32, same shape/transform as the sample), set the submission name and an optional note of at most 140 characters (E2’s recorded note, for provenance only: “S24 CCKM cross-contact kurtosis B14/B13 37k dots; research only, not slot-approved”), and create the submission.
  4. Save the submission ID, timestamp, uploaded-file SHA-256, and resulting score together. Only that receipt may later be labelled ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Known irregularity: the official text says data outside bounds should be null/NaN, while the fleet’s scored “-zeros” files use 0 outside with nodata=None; server behaviour is unverified from here, so the selector must make the final encoding call explicitly.

Method in one paragraph

CCKM (bands B14 total magnetic intensity + B13 isostatic gravity only): masked 15-pixel-window excess kurtosis of the B14 high-pass residual, sampled ±9 px across the B13 gradient normal and gated by |∇B13| — a change in magnetic texture tail-weight across a density contact, targeting faults buried under cover with no mapped scarp. Named mimics: unfaulted intrusive/volcanic contacts, lava-flow margins, survey seams. Declared-NoData cells (3,061 per band) are excluded from every statistic (masked normalised convolution) — the repair of the S22 failure mode. Band identities are internally corroborated label-blind (B03 vs |∇B14|: r = 1.000000 on 5,135,228 eroded-valid pixels; receipt). A fault map is not evidence of a geothermal vent or reservoir.
