## 1. Why `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) Led the Fleet — and Its Structural Ceiling

Across the entire `GEMSDOE` fleet (`GEMSDOE` through `GEMSDOE92`), the highest organizer-confirmed public leaderboard score is **`0.2778`** (`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros`, `37,654` binary unit-mass dots), achieved via a four-stage progression under the competition's Distance-Tolerant Tversky Index ($\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, $300\text{ m}$ linear triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(0, 1 - d / 3\text{ px})$):

$$\text{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{T}{0.2\,T + 0.2\,(S - M) + 0.8\,|G|}, \qquad T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{j:\,\|j-g\|_2 \le 3} p_j\,k(\|j-g\|_2)$$

1. **Multi-physics coincidence (`H19-5`, `121,131` px $\to$ `0.1922` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED):** Required structural tip/relay proximity (`L1`), GDR thermal/geochemical upflow (`L2`), 3DEP topographic break (`L3`), and horizontal potential-field worm (`L4`) simultaneously, eliminating single-layer mimics.
2. **2-D Poisson disk thinning (`d >= 2.8 px`, `44,090` dots $\to$ `0.2600` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED):** Under $\max_j$ kernel credit (`scripts/dti.py`), a 3-pixel-wide predicted ribbon contributes $1\times$ to $T$ and $3\times$ to $S$, tripling $(S - M)$ false-positive mass with zero recall gain. Thinning to $\ge 280\text{ m}$ dot spacing boosted DTI by **`+0.0678`**.
3. **Isolated-speckle pruning (`solo`, `40,199` dots $\to$ `0.2708` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED):** Removed isolated points lacking strike continuity within $900\text{ m}$, lifting DTI by **`+0.0108`**.
4. **Known-catalogue collar deletion (`d_cat > 2.0 px`, `37,654` dots $\to$ `0.2778` ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED):** Because DrivenData scores predictions exclusively on **unmapped/withheld** faults while `existing_faults.tif` (`60,988` pixels) carries zero positive test credit, deleting `2,545` near-catalogue dots removed pure $(S - M)$ deadweight for **`+0.0070`**.

### Why R0 Perturbations (`GEMSDOE90`–`GEMSDOE92`) Cannot Break `0.3195`–`0.4413`
Every prior candidate in `GEMSDOE91` (`50` builds in `ledger/submissions.csv`) is a post-hoc morphological edit of `R0` (`80%–100%` 3-px dot overlap with `R0`). Relocating or pruning `37,654` pre-frozen dots cannot discover a single blind intrabasinal geothermal fault that `H19-5` missed. Specifically, `H19-5` and subsequent sibling lanes (`GEMSDOE36`, `GEMSDOE70`, `GEMSDOE76`) left four quantitative physical errors on the table:

1. **Plan-view Dip-Offset Error ($200\text{ m}$–$1,000\text{ m} > 300\text{ m}$ DTI kernel):** Great Basin normal faults dip at $\delta \approx 50^\circ\text{–}60^\circ$ into the hanging-wall basin. Where alluvial/playa fill thickness (`Band 15: depth_to_base_surf`) is $H = 300\text{–}1,400\text{ m}$, the maximum basement density/susceptibility contrast lies $H \cot \delta \approx 2.1\text{–}10.0\text{ px}$ **downdip** of the sub-alluvial fault trace. Unshifted gravity/magnetic worms miss the $3\text{ px}$ ($300\text{ m}$) DTI kernel completely.
2. **Unsigned `tc` Band Misinterpretation:** `Band 6` (`tc`, `[11.8, 25.6]` mGal terrain correction) is strictly positive and slightly *lower* on catalogue faults (`17.4` vs `18.5`) — it is **not** a signed Verduzco/Miller-Singh potential-field tilt angle. True depth-normalized Verduzco tilt must be synthesized from signed `Band 11` (`iso_grav_anom_vg`, which flips sign across fault contacts: `+0.082` on faults vs `-0.074` background) divided by unsigned `Band 5` (`iso_grav_anom_slope`), and `Band 9` (`rtp_vd`) divided by $|\nabla_H \text{Band 2}|$.
3. **212 Discarded Blind Geothermometer Conduits (`IR-91-36`):** `src/gems91/prior.py` filters `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` strictly on `thermalclass in {'Hot', 'Warm'}` (plus drops `'Hot '` rows with trailing whitespace unless stripped), discarding **`212` unique footprint grid cells** where `Cold`/`NULL` wells exhibit dissolved quartz, chalcedony, or Na-K-Ca chemical geothermometry $\ge 90^\circ\text{C}$ — the textbook geochemical signature of **blind** fault-hosted hydrothermal upflow cooled conductively in shallow aquifers (Coolbaugh et al. 2006; Faulds & Hinz 2015).
4. **Exposure-Confounded Positive-Unlabeled (PU) Labels:** `existing_faults.tif` is heavily biased toward exposed range fronts (`slope` median `11.1°` vs `3.7°` background; `depth_to_base_surf` median `375 m` vs `667 m`). Naive binary classifiers learn range-front bedrock exposure rather than fault physics, starving sub-alluvial geothermal basins of probability mass.

## 3. Detailed Physical Specifications & Mimic Rejection

### `H91-1` (Rank 1 — Experiment `E1`): Dip-Deprojected Verduzco Tilt + Multi-Scale Openness + Submodular 300 m DTI Allocator
- **Exact Data Layers:** Official competition bands `B02 (rtp)`, `B05 (iso_grav_anom_slope)`, `B09 (rtp_vd)`, `B11 (iso_grav_anom_vg)`, `B12 (det_elev)`, `B13 (iso_grav_anom)`, `B15 (depth_to_base_surf)`, `B17 (cond_surf)`, `B19 (slope)`, plus all 19 cleaned base bands (`< -1e30` sentinel filled via nearest-finite EDT).
- **Physical Signature & Mathematical Transform:**
  1. **True Signed Verduzco / Miller-Singh Tilt Angles** (Miller & Singh 1994; Verduzco et al. 2004):
     $$\theta_{\text{grav}} = \arctan2\!\big(\text{B11},\,\max(\text{B05}, 0) + 10^{-4}\big), \qquad \theta_{\text{mag}} = \arctan2\!\big(\text{B09},\,\|\nabla_H \text{B02}\|_2 + 10^{-3}\big)$$
     Because $\text{B11}$ and $\text{B05}$ decay at identical $(z + z_0)^{-(n+1)}$ rates with burial depth $z_0$, their ratio equalizes deep sub-basin basement steps ($H = 1\text{ km}$) with shallow range fronts ($H = 50\text{ m}$), placing $\theta_{\text{grav}} = 0$ directly above the basement fault contact and maximizing $\|\nabla_H \theta_{\text{grav}}\|_2$ along strike.
  2. **Vector Updip Basement-to-Surface De-Projection:** Normal faults dip down the smoothed regional isostatic gravity gradient $\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{down}} = -\nabla G_6(\text{B13}) / \|\nabla G_6(\text{B13})\|_2$ (from high-density footwall horst into low-density hanging-wall basin). For each candidate surface trace $(y, x)$ under basin fill $H = \max(\text{B15}, 0)$, we sample $\exp(-|\theta_{\text{grav}}|/0.25)$, $\|\nabla_H \theta_{\text{grav}}\|_2$, and $\|\nabla_H \theta_{\text{mag}}\|_2$ at the downdip basement pierce point $(y, x) + \operatorname{clip}(H \cot 55^\circ / 100\text{ m},\,0,\,10\text{ px})\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{down}}$ via bilinear `scipy.ndimage.map_coordinates`.
  3. **Multi-Scale Topographic Openness & Structure-Tensor Coherence:** $300\text{ m}$ and $1\text{ km}$ Local Relief Models ($\text{B12} - G_\sigma(\text{B12})$), topographic Laplacian openness $-\nabla^2 G_2(\text{B12})$, and $400\text{ m}$ elevation structure-tensor anisotropy $(\lambda_1 - \lambda_2)/(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2)$.
  4. **Exact 300 m Submodular Marginal-DTI Lazy-Greedy Allocator (`submodular_dti_emit`):** Directly maximizes expected competition numerator $\sum_x q(x) \max_{s \in S} k(\|x-s\|_2)$ minus marginal false-positive penalty across exact $7 \times 7$ ($r \le 3.0\text{ px}$) neighborhoods via Minoux (1978) lazy-greedy priority queue, strictly enforcing $d(\text{existing\_faults}) > 3.0\text{ px}$.
- **Why It Catches Faults Missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** USGS Qfaults maps subaerial scarps; concealed range-front step-faults and intrabasinal horst boundaries buried beneath $200\text{–}1,200\text{ m}$ of Quaternary Lahontan/Humboldt alluvium have zero surface scarp and had their gravity/magnetic gradients displaced $2\text{–}10\text{ px}$ outside the $3\text{ px}$ DTI kernel in all un-deprojected models.
- **Named Non-Fault Mimic & Rejection:** **Pleistocene Lake Lahontan wave-cut shorelines** (`det_elev` / `slope` steps at constant `1,330 m` paleolake highstands) produce crisp topographic breaks with zero updip-deprojected basement Verduzco tilt (`updip_grav_tilt_hd` $\approx 0$).
- **Differentiation from Repo Work:** First-principles discovery (not an `R0` edit); unlike `GEMSDOE76` (which applied unshifted isotropic horizontal tilt worms without `B15` dip de-projection), `H91-1` projects basement potential-field edges updip along $-\nabla G_6(\text{B13})$ by $H \cot 55^\circ$.
