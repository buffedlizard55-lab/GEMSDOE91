Why you saw “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” before

The competition form rejects any pixel outside [0, 1], and NaN fails that check too (NaN is not in range). Several historical GEMSDOE artifacts are -nan rasters (1.0 at dots, NaN outside the footprint) or HTML/JSON audit pages — any of these triggers the error.
