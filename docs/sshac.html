METHOD

What this detector is

Unsupervised geometric mean of 6 catalogue-free terms, each as a footprint percentile [0,1]:

  1. Seismic proximity = 1 - pct(B10 deq_n100a15) — close to micro-earthquake epicenters = high
  2. Strain magnitude = pct(B04 geod_2ndinv) — decadal second invariant from GPS/InSAR
  3. Radiometric edge = pct(|grad(B06 tc)|) — total-count gamma-ray edge (K/Th/U) after EDT fill
  4. Magnetic ridge = pct(high-pass( (B03+B06+|B09|)/3 )) — 500 m high-pass concordance
  5. Gravity ridge = pct(high-pass( (B05+|B11|+|B18|)/3 ))
  6. Topo ridge = pct(high-pass( (B19+|lap(B12)|)/2 ))

Surface = (Π_i term_i)^(1/6) — high only where all six coincide after 200–500 m smoothing. Emission: greedy spaced 2.8 px, 37,654 dots, >3 px from catalogue (full for final, >3 px from visible in each holdout fold). No parent raster read; no distance-to-catalogue feature in detector.

Named non-fault mimic: buried volcanic margin (strong B06/B03 but aseismic, low strain) → rejected by requiring seismic AND strain coincidence. Playa conductive fill (B17 high but low strain/seismic) similarly suppressed. Implementation: src/gems91/ss_hac.py + scripts/build_ss_simple.py + scripts/build_ss_final.py.
