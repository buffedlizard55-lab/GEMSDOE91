### 1.2 Exact Mathematical Mechanism of `GEMSDOE32` (`R0`, `37,654` Dots) Under Distance-Weighted Tversky Index
In Professor John Lipor's official [`drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution`](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution) (Cell 16: `alpha = 0.2`, `beta = 0.8`) and this repository's verified scorer `scripts/dti.py`, predictions $p(x) \in [0, 1]$ on the `EPSG:32611` `3730 × 3292` ($100\text{ m}$) grid are scored against unmapped ground-truth fault pixels $G$ via the $R = 300\text{ m}$ ($3\text{ px}$) linear triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d/3, 0)$:

$$\text{DTI}(0.2, 0.8) = \frac{T}{0.2\,T + 0.2\,(S - M) + 0.8\,|G| + \epsilon}$$

where:
- $T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{x:\,d(x,g)\le 3} p(x)\,k(d(x,g))$ (**max-kernel recall credit**, not additive sum),
- $S = \sum_x p(x)$ (total emitted prediction mass), and
- $M = \sum_{x:\,p(x)>0} p(x)\,\max_{g \in G} k(d(x,g))$ (proximity-credited prediction mass, so $\text{FP}_w = S - M$).

Byte-verified lineage forensics (`docs/parent-forensics.json`) establish why the four transformations from `19GEMSDOE` to `GEMSDOE32` (`R0`) monotonically improved precision-per-unit-mass under this functional:
1. **Multi-Physics Coincidence (`H19-5`, `121,131` px, `0.1922` user-reported `CLAIM`):** Required simultaneous structural, topographic, potential-field, and thermal/geochemical support, suppressing single-modality false positives.
2. **Poisson-Disk Thinning to $d \ge 2.8\text{ px}$ (`GEMSDOE25`, `44,090` dots, `0.2600` user-reported `CLAIM`):** Because $T$ applies a pointwise $\max$ over $d \le 3\text{ px}$, a 3-pixel-wide ribbon increases $S$ by $3\times$ while leaving $T$ nearly unchanged. Enforcing $d \ge 2.8\text{ px}$ eliminated redundant $(S - M)$ mass.
3. **0-D Speckle Removal (`GEMSDOE28` `solo`, `40,199` dots, `0.2708` user-reported `CLAIM`):** Removed `3,891` isolated dots lacking collinear fault neighbors within $900\text{ m}$; real faults are 1-D planar traces, whereas isolated 100-m dots are geomorphic/potential-field noise.
4. **Known-Catalogue Collar Deletion (`GEMSDOE32` `h33-2-b2`, `37,654` dots, `0.2778` user-reported `CLAIM`):** Removed `2,545` dots within $200\text{ m}$ of `existing_faults.tif` (`60,988` pixels), preventing false-positive waste on already-mapped faults.
