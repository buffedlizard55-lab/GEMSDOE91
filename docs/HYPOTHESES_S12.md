### 1.2 Exact Convex-Analysis Proof: Why Sparse Binary `{0, 1}` Dots Crushed Soft Continuous Ribbons (`0.018–0.189` → `0.2778`)
Under the official [DrivenData Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) (`vendor/gems57/metric.py` & `scripts/dti.py`), predictions $p \in [0, 1]^N$ are scored on hidden newly mapped faults $G$ using the **Distance-Weighted Tversky Index** ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8$, triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d/R, 0)$ with radius $R = 300\text{ m} = 3\text{ px}$ at $100\text{ m}$ grid spacing):

$$\text{DTI}(p) = \frac{\text{TP}_w(p)}{\text{TP}_w(p) + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w(p) + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w(p)} = \frac{T(p)}{0.2\,T(p) + 0.2\,(S(p) - M(p)) + 0.8\,|G|}$$

where:
- $T(p) = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{j:\,d(j,g)\le 3} p_j\,k(d(j,g))$ is max-pooled kernel truth credit,
- $S(p) = \sum_{j=1}^N p_j$ is total emitted mass, and
- $M(p) = \sum_{j=1}^N p_j \max_{g \in G} k(d(j,g))$ is proximity-credited prediction mass, so $\text{FP}_w(p) = \sum_{j=1}^N p_j(1 - m_j)$.

**Theorem 1 (Coordinate-Wise Convexity & Extreme-Point Optimality of DTI):**
Fix any operating score $\lambda = \text{DTI}^* \in [0, 1]$ and any posterior probability law $\mathbb{P}(G \mid \mathcal{D})$ over unknown fault sets $G$. By Dinkelbach's fractional-programming equivalence, maximizing $\text{DTI}(p)$ is governed by the linearized functional:

$$L_\lambda(p) = (1 - 0.2\lambda)\,\mathbb{E}_G\!\left[\sum_{g \in G} \max_{j} \bigl(p_j\,k_{jg}\bigr)\right] - 0.2\lambda \sum_{j=1}^N p_j\,\bigl(1 - \mathbb{E}_G[m_j]\bigr) - 0.8\lambda\,\mathbb{E}_G[|G|].$$

Hold all pixels $p_{-i}$ fixed and vary a single pixel $p_i \in [0, 1]$:
1. For every fault configuration $G$ and every $g \in G$, $h_g(p_i) = \max\!\bigl(\max_{j \ne i} p_j k_{jg},\; p_i k_{ig}\bigr)$ is the pointwise maximum of a constant and a non-negative linear function of $p_i$, hence **convex** on $[0, 1]$ (zero slope until $p_i$ exceeds existing neighbor coverage, positive slope $k_{ig}$ thereafter).
2. Because $1 - 0.2\lambda \ge 0.8 > 0$ and expectation $\mathbb{E}_G[\cdot]$ preserves convexity, the first term is convex in $p_i$, while the second term $-0.2\lambda\,p_i(1 - \mathbb{E}_G[m_i])$ is **linear** in $p_i$.
3. Every coordinate-wise convex function on the compact hypercube $[0, 1]^N$ attains its global maximum at an **extreme point (vertex) $p^* \in \{0, 1\}^N$**.

This proves rigorously why `GEMSDOE32` (`R0`, `37,654` binary `{0, 1}` dots) outperformed every soft U-Net / continuous-probability submission (`20GEMSDOE` `0.1859`, `58GEMSDOE` `0.0180`):

2. **Elimination of Within-Kernel Crowding ($d \ge 2.8\text{ px} \approx R$):** In `19GEMSDOE` (`121,131` px, `0.1922`), 3-pixel-wide ridge ribbons contributed $3\times$ to $S(p)$ and $1\times$ to max-pooled $T(p)$.
