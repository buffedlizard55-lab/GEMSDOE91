Prior result: a plausible mechanism, not demonstrated optimality

Comparing pinned parent and ancestor rasters verifies a deletion-only lineage: the old parent removed predictions close to known catalogue pixels. Sparse placement can reduce redundant mass under max-kernel truth credit. But deletion can also lose credit on real continuations. A hidden-truth score receipt or properly regenerated paired holdout is required to identify which mechanism dominated.

The cached GEMSDOE32 page states “NO ORGANISER SCORE EXISTS for this or any artifact in this repository.” Consequently, claims that its spacing was theoretically optimal, that regional strain would beat it, or that model-based deltas were live scores are withdrawn from the active portal.

The legacy local metric summed overlapping truth credit and could exceed one. It is now an adapter to the pinned shared max-credit implementation; obsolete model forecasts remain historical records, not promotion evidence.
