Why GEMSDOE32's 0.2778 may be high

The plausible explanation is metric-aware thinning, not proof of a new geological detector: the owner-maintained lineage describes H19-5 ridge pixels thinned to roughly 2.8-pixel spacing, followed by catalogue-flank deletions. If close-by predictions have overlapping 300 m kernel credit, while each prediction still adds false-positive mass, pruning low-yield pixels can improve a distance-weighted Tversky-style score. A public/private evaluation that masks already-mapped catalogue pixels could make the near-catalogue ring especially inefficient.

What is not proved: the reported score has no verified receipt-to-byte association in the audited records; the rasters were not re-evaluated under a shared controlled experiment here; and “zero-credit” is not established for hidden truth. The mechanism is plausible, not causal attribution. The score is not evidence of geothermal-vent discovery.

Can it be beaten? Possible in principle; no evidence from this checkout that it can. No new candidate was authorized or tested. The best file to submit from this review is none.
