Mechanism analysis · not causal proof

Why GEMSDOE32's reported value may be highest in this family

A plausible metric-geometric explanation

The pinned byte-lineage audit in R0_ANALYSIS_S22.md places the GEMSDOE32 file inside the same H19-derived support family. It reports that the GEMSDOE32 raster adds no positive pixel beyond the earlier D2.8 support and removes a substantial near-catalogue subset. Under the official DTI definition, false positives receive weight alpha=0.2, false negatives beta=0.8, and distances are softened within 300 m. If the removed dots were mostly unmatched clutter while retained detections still reached hidden faults, the weighted false-positive cost could decrease while true-positive credit stayed similar.

This explains how pruning could accompany a higher reported value; it does not prove the value or identify the hidden truth.

Why it is not an independent win claim

The nine-site audit reports that GEMSDOE32's positive support has 100.00% maximum overlap within 3 px with GEMSDOE24. That is peer-family similarity, not independent discovery. The historical source register records an owner-page statement that no organizer score existed for the named artifact at that time; it was not re-fetched today. No current receipt ties the reported number to the bytes.

Accordingly, the table preserves the value only as an unverified user-reported public claim. No alternative leaderboard score is estimated here.

Submission guide

  1. Get the assigned lane. Obtain one authorized method paragraph and its source; do not select one of the proposals yourself.
  2. Restore and verify the data. Obtain the official competition stack and sample through the authorized data page; verify all manifest hashes and resolve sample semantics using organizer-controlled bytes.
  3. Preregister and reuse. Follow the shared cached stack, evaluator, and writer. Keep whole-segment buffered hide-and-recover and pooled DTI alpha=0.2, beta=0.8, 300 m. Stay within three experiments or two hours.
  4. Run leakage and uniqueness stops. Feature-alone canaries first. Stop if any pre-placement registry surface rank correlation is greater than 0.90; after placement stop if more than 70% of candidate dots are within 3 px of any available registry raster.
  5. Validate the actual TIFF automatically. Verify single-band float32, exact grid/CRS/transform, [0,1] range and required outside-bounds null/NaN behavior, then run the complete registry comparison. Format validity is not scientific merit or selector approval.
  6. Wait for the separate selector decision. Do not upload automatically. If and only if selected, use the official DrivenData submission page and archive the exact submitted bytes and receipt. Only that receipt can support ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.
