### 2.2 Exact Mathematical & Physical Mechanism Behind `0.1922 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778` (`USER-REPORTED`)
The competition metric ([DrivenData Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/); [Official Reference Solution](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution); [NLR Prize Rules PDF](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf)) is the **Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (DTI)** with $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, and a linear $300\text{ m}$ ($R = 3\text{ px}$ at $100\text{ m/px}$ in [EPSG:32611](https://epsg.io/32611)) triangular kernel $K(\Delta y, \Delta x) = \max(0, 1 - \sqrt{\Delta y^2 + \Delta x^2}/3)$:

$$\mathrm{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{T}{T + \alpha\,\mathrm{FP} + \beta\,\mathrm{FN}} = \frac{T}{0.2\,T + 0.2\,(S - M) + 0.8\,|G|}$$

where:
- $G \subset \Omega$ is the set of unmapped (hidden) ground-truth fault pixels (mapped catalogue faults in `existing_faults.tif` are masked out before scoring),
- $T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{y \in \Omega} \left[ P(y)\,K(g - y) \right]$ is the distance-weighted true-positive truth coverage,
- $S = \sum_{y \in \Omega} P(y)$ is the total predicted mass,
- $M = \sum_{y \in \Omega} P(y) \max_{g \in G} K(y - g)$ is the matched prediction credit, so $\mathrm{FP} = S - M$ and $\mathrm{FN} = |G| - T$.

Byte-exact inspection across the four pinned lineage stages reveals three mathematical mechanisms that drove the score progression:

1. **Step 1 — Multi-Modal Habitat Corroboration (`19GEMSDOE` `h19-5`: `0.1922 USER-REPORTED`):**

   Because $T$ takes a **pointwise $\max_{y}$** over the $R = 3\text{ px}$ ($300\text{ m}$) disk while $\mathrm{FP} = S - M$ sums $P(y)$ **linearly**, two adjacent $1.0$ pixels along a continuous fault trace cover almost the exact same $3\text{ px}$ disk of hidden fault truth ($\Delta T \approx 0$) while doubling the false-positive penalty $0.2\,S$ wherever the predicted trace lies $> 300\text{ m}$ from hidden truth!
